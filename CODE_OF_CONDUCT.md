# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

LingoFlux contributors and maintainers are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community. Participation should make the project safer, clearer, and more useful for people of every background and level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar project constraints.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's credentials, private text, identity, or security details.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, safety, store compliance, and project direction.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized language, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately introducing malicious code, credential collection, hidden tracking, or misleading store behavior.
- Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/xuanquangIT). Do not include sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as practical, with privacy respected to the extent possible.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.
